Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit this website or contact Sarah Hartwell about coaching services.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Information that may be collected

You may choose to provide your name, email address, and details included in an enquiry. The website host may also receive basic technical information, such as an IP address, browser type, device type, and requested pages, through standard server logs.

Contact and enquiries

This website does not contain an online form. If you use an email link to request information or a consultation, the information you send is processed through your email provider and the recipient's email provider.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

Cookies and analytics

The current website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or non-essential cookies. The hosting provider may use strictly necessary technology or server logs to deliver and protect the website.

How information may be used

Information may be used to:

  • respond to enquiries and consultation requests;
  • communicate about services you have asked about;
  • operate, maintain, and secure the website; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations.

Third-party services

Limited information may be handled by providers needed to host and deliver the website or email communications. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Information is not sold through this website.

Data retention

Enquiry information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant records, or meet legal obligations. Technical logs are retained according to the hosting provider's practices.

Data security

Reasonable care is taken when handling personal information. However, no website, email system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about, access, correct, or delete personal information. You may contact Sarah using the email below. A request may need to be verified before it can be completed.

External links

Links that open another website or service are governed by that provider's own terms and privacy practices. This policy does not control third-party websites.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the website or its information practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to sarahhartwell@gmail.com.
